Shout brings the room online: a class, a stream, a meeting, or a game night.
Guests join with one link, install nothing, and need no account.

## The apps

- **Arena:** questions and raised hands from every phone; the host chooses what
  the room sees.
- **Poll:** ask a question and watch the votes arrive, live.
- **Quiz Desk:** quiz the room on their phones; nobody sees the answer until it
  is revealed.
- **Tier List** and **Kanban:** rank, plan, and agree on one live board, with
  planning poker built in.
- **Scoreboard:** keep score for any game or league, and show it anywhere.
- **Tabletop:** chess, cards, ludo, and more, with dice and a chat.
- **Chat** and **Paint:** talk and draw together between the big moments.

Every app runs on [Thuum.dev](/projects/thuum/) brokers, and can be added to
OBS as a browser source for live streams. No ads, no trackers, and guest data
is never sold.
