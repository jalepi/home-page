Every technology you use has a story: a rivalry, a mistake, a stubborn idea, a
bet that shouldn't have paid off. Story of Things digs into the history of the
tools developers use every day, and turns each one into something worth
listening to on your commute, at the gym, or wherever your mind wanders.

Each season takes on a new "thing." Season five, for example, follows Ruby from
its origins through Rails, the "Does Rails scale?" saga, and its influence on
Elixir and Phoenix.

## How it's made

Story of Things is produced **without any AI services**. Every episode comes
out of my own Python pipelines, orchestrating AI agents built on open-source
software and open-weight LLMs, running **completely locally**.
